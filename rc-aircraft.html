01

Overview

A 5.5 ft tail-dragger that won the RC3 competition with a 3 min 40 s flight. The design was iterated through several prototypes over a few months.

My role
Co-founder and design member; designed the wing, tail and booms, and worked on the fuselage and the horizontal and vertical stabilizers; ran the landing-gear FEA and tests, integrated the flight controller and radio, helped wire the vision payload (built by a teammate)
