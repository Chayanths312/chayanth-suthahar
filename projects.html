FRC 2056 · Mechanical · 01

Drivetrain.

A 32" × 28" swerve base for the 2025 World Champion robot, geared for 15 ft/s

Image coming soonCAD render of the 2056 swerve drive base with electronicsdrivebase.png
Image coming soonMachining drivetrain parts in the shopmachining.jpg

Features

  • 3" × 1" × 1/8" aluminum box tube as main structure
    • Stiff, low chassis to keep the centre of mass down
  • 1/16" solid aluminum baseplate
    • Doubles as shear plate and electronics tray
    • Corners bolted to back up the rivets
    • Riveted aluminum angle near the battery stops sag
  • SDS MK4i modules with Kraken X60 drive at the L2 ratio for 15 ft/s
    • Balances speed and acceleration for the game's short cycles
  • Custom shifted adapter plate for Kraken X44 steering with a 12-tooth pinion
    • Saved nearly 2 lb, keeping the 135 lb robot under the weight limit
  • Baseplate fastened directly to the elevator gearbox
Result
2025 FIRST World Champion and Ontario Champion; team 1st in Canada 2022–2025
My role
Designed the drive base, gearboxes and mounts; machined baseplates, gearbox plates, shafts and motor mounts on mill, lathe and CNC

RC3 Competition · Aerospace · 02

RC Aircraft.

A 5.5 ft tail-dragger that won the RC3 competition with a 3 min 40 s flight

Image coming soonFinished RC aircraft with its covered airframeplane-covered.png
Image coming soonRC aircraft with the covering removed, showing the ribs and sparsplane-uncovered.png

Drag to rotate

Drag sideways, or use the left and right arrow keys, to rotate the aircraft.

Features

  • 5.5 ft tapered wing on aluminum spars
    • Ribs pocketed to cut weight without losing stiffness
  • 3D-printed fuselage with three carbon fibre tail booms
  • Aluminum tail-dragger landing gear validated with static FEA
    • Geometry iterated against touchdown-impact results
    • Redesign confirmed over 5 impacts: three 1 m drop tests and two real landings
  • SpeedyBee F405 Wing flight controller running iNav, ELRS radio link
  • Onboard Pi Zero vision payload
    • Scored the 100-point computer vision objective
Result
1st place on longest flight time (3:40, flown until the battery drained); judges' Best Handling aircraft
My role
Co-founder and design member; ran the landing-gear FEA and tests, integrated the flight controller and radio, helped wire the vision payload (built by a teammate)

MakeUofT 2026 · Robotics · 03

VitalGroove.

A vision-triggered candy dispenser built and demoed in 24 hours

Image coming soonThe VitalGroove candy dispenser buildvitalgroove-build.jpg

Features

  • Jetson Nano vision pipeline detects a hug in real time
    • Captures a polaroid-style photo of the moment
    • VLM pipeline decides when to dispense
  • Serial link from the Jetson to an ESP32
    • A detection fires the dispenser instantly
  • Servo-actuated gate dispenser
  • ESP32 with I2C sensors, wiring and power brought up from scratch
Result
Working live demo inside the 24-hour limit
My role
Designed and built the dispenser and led hardware integration

Gibberish Hackathon · Software · 04

EdgeWave.

A browser instrument you play with hand gestures — built in 2 hours, 1st place

Video coming soonEdgeWave demo: playing chords and drums with hand gestures in the browseredgewave.mp4 · poster edgewave-poster.jpg

Features

  • MediaPipe Hands tracks both hands through the webcam
    • No physical controller needed
  • Right hand plays chords
    • Fist gestures and palm height map to diatonic chords in all 12 keys
    • Palm position sweeps a low-pass filter from 300 Hz to 8 kHz
  • Left hand is a 4-piece drum kit, one finger per instrument
    • Wrist tilt sets repeat speed, roughly 65–500 BPM
  • Timestamp-based looper for layering a performance hands-free
    • Debounced chord changes and stale-hand detection release notes cleanly when tracking drops
Result
1st place, Gibberish hackathon, built in 2 hours
My role
Built the majority of the app on a team of 3 (JavaScript, MediaPipe)
